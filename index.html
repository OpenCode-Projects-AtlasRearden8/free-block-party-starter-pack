How to use it

  • Print: any printer, regular 8.5×11 paper. Each printable has a Print button; blanks not filled in the browser print as dotted lines for pen.
  • Edit: click any dotted blank and type. This runs entirely on your device — no server, no analytics, no cookies, nothing submitted anywhere.
  • Crash course: print the flyer first, then the organizer reminders, then whatever else the evening needs.
One small ask: check your city's website for a block-party permit before you pick a date — lead times range from none to four weeks. The kind reminders page covers all of this gently.
