Mini plan B by category

If…Then…
Light drizzleCanopies and patience. Games move under the porch; potluck proceeds. Kids' raincoats are honored and praised.
Steady rainFull move to the indoor spot above. Serving focus shifts to cold sides and warm mains held in slow cookers — transport with care, label as always.
ThunderstormCancel today, pick a rain date one week later, and let neighbors vote by text. No heroics.
Extreme heatShift the party to after 6 pm, add the sponge water game, extra water stations, and shade canopies.
Gusty windWeight the canopies, tape down the table cards, and skip the thin-string balloons entirely.

What changes indoors

Kid games: cup stacking derby + neighborhood bingo adapt perfectly indoors
Chalk gallery becomes paper gallery (big paper, markers, same ribbons)
Grass area becomes "find a neighbor who…" bingo again — it travels well
Cleanup is half the size; thank the indoor spot's owners
Indoor host etiquette: shoes off at the door if asked, food only at the food table, and leave the space better than we found it.
