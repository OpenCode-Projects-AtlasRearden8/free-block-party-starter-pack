Kid Games — Olympics

Chalk + cups + cones = a whole afternoon. Zero cost, mild noise, full joy.

1. The Great Sock Race (ages ~4–10, 10 min)

Kids hop from cone to cone with a beanbag (or a sock full of rice) balanced on a paper plate held out like a waiter. Dropped beanbag = one laughing squat, then keep going. Everyone gets a cheer; fastest gets a bragging-right certificate doodled in chalk.

2. Chalk Art Gallery (all ages, ongoing)

Reserve a driveway stretch as "the gallery." Chalk with a theme each hour — animals, dream houses, portraits of the neighbor with the grill. Judges are encouraged to award ribbons made of paper towels and confidence.

3. Cup Stacking Derby (ages ~5+, 5 min heats)

Two teams. Each team builds the tallest tower from plastic cups, then the whole team must walk it all down without toppling it. Guaranteed screaming. Guaranteed laughter.

4. Sponge Buccaneers (hot days, all ages)

Two buckets per team, one line between them. Kids ferry water using a big sponge head-to-head. Winner is whoever soaked the fewest adults.
